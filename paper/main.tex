The \sysid{} pipeline studied here originates from the ForzaETH Race Stack and
the work of Dikici \emph{et al.}~\cite{ontrack2025}, released under an
open-source licence; this paper reports a port, a correction, and an extension
of that work rather than a reimplementation from scratch.

\section*{Ethics}
All results in this paper are obtained in simulation. No experiment involved
human subjects, personal data, or the operation of a physical vehicle, so no
human-subjects approval and no vehicle-safety protocol were required.

\section*{Data and Code Availability}
The identification package, the ROS~2 bridge, the MPC and the benchmarking
packages, the scenario definitions, the exported result CSVs behind every
figure and table of Section~\ref{sec:results}, and the sweep generator behind
Table~\ref{tab:sweep} are in the repositories listed in
Appendix~\ref{sec:refimpl}; the top-level project repository is
\url{https://github.com/ebrahimabdelghfar/Ebrahim_Master_Thesis_repo/tree/feat-follow-premade-racing-track}. \sysid{} is an extension of the ForzaETH release
of~\cite{ontrack2025} and is distributed under the MIT licence it declares in
its package manifest; \bridge{} is GPL-3.0. The two
are used as separate ROS~2 overlays communicating only over ROS~2 topics and
services, and neither links against the other, so no combined-work obligation
arises between them; the identification and control packages in this work are
released under the MIT terms the \sysid{} upstream requires.
